# NPC profile: the bridge surveyor

[Back to gallery](./README.md)

**Resource:** [NPC Creator](../../campaign-creation/agents/npc-creator.md).
**Audience:** DM preparation. **Status:** Draft followed by a simulated
DM-reviewed example. All material is original fiction; no real review or
campaign canon is claimed.

## Input

> Create a noncombat NPC for a hopeful D&D 5e campaign in the invented town of
> Reedcross. Oren Pell is a dwarf bridge surveyor who needs volunteers to check
> faded route markers. Give me the agent's profile format and two optional
> hooks. Keep conflict non-graphic. Do not invent player relationships or
> decisions. No hidden history has been established; label new details as
> proposals, not facts about an existing campaign.

## Illustrative output: draft

All details below are proposals except the supplied name, ancestry, profession,
location, and need for help with markers.

### Identity

**Oren Pell**, a dwarf of about 90, works as Reedcross's bridge surveyor.
Chalk dust whitens his cuffs; his coat has oversized pockets, and his brass
measuring chain is neatly coiled. He has no assigned combat class or stat block.

### Personality

Oren treats careful maintenance as a form of hospitality.

- **Traits:** Checks a measurement twice; thanks people for pointing out errors.
- **Ideal:** Public paths should be usable by everyone.
- **Bond:** The walking routes connecting Reedcross's outlying homes.
- **Flaw:** His insistence on perfect records can delay a useful temporary fix.

### Story

**Public backstory, proposed:** Oren learned surveying while helping repair
footpaths after seasonal floods.

**Private truth:** Not established by the input; leave open for DM development.

**Secret:** None established. Do not turn an omitted history into a revelation.

### Roleplaying

He speaks in short, practical questions: "Where does the path stop making
sense? Show me that bit first." He taps his chain against his palm when
thinking and steps aside so others can examine his drawings.

**Flattery:** Redirects praise toward the repair crew.
**Threats:** Ends the discussion and seeks help without escalating.
**Coin:** Accepts payment for materials, not preferential access.
**Kindness:** Offers a warm seat and listens without interrupting.

### Adventure hooks

1. **Faded markers:** Oren asks for a daylight survey. The party can map the
   route, seek a local guide, or decline; he can offer supplies and a fair
   payment to be set by the DM.
2. **Two useful paths:** Two lanes each need the last available replacement
   sign. The party could consult residents, make another sign, or help Oren
   arrange a temporary marker rather than pick a winner.

**Immediate request:** Help identify where travelers lose the marked route.
**Possible return:** A town map and introductions to local craftspeople;
neither benefit is guaranteed until the DM accepts it.

## Simulated DM review

**Accept:** Oren's appearance, mannerisms, and the faded-marker request.
These are now established only within this example.

**Change:** Oren lends surveying supplies and a town map; remove any promised
payment until the campaign's reward scale is known.

**Defer:** The second hook and all unwritten history remain proposals.
No character is assumed to volunteer or already know Oren.

**Sharing decision:** DM-reviewed does not mean release-ready. Prepare a
separate introduction using only Oren's visible appearance and spoken request
if the party meets him. Do not share the profile or review as a player handout.
