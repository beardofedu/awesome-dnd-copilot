Table safety and care

This public page explains general procedures only. Discuss personal boundaries privately, outside this site and its repository.

Pause or change a scene

Agree on a simple word, gesture, or accessible signal to pause play. When someone uses it, pause and adjust without requiring a personal explanation.

Take a break

Agree that anyone may step away or leave a session without pressure to explain. Choose a way to resume play that does not exclude the returning player.

Keep checking in

Revisit the group's procedures when needed. An earlier agreement is not permanent permission, and silence is not consent.

Keep personal information private

Do not publish named preferences, disclosures, reasons for a boundary, or private messages. Share only general procedures the group has approved for public use.
