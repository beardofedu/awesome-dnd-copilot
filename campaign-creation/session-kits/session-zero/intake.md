# Session-zero intake

Work through each stage together; skip or defer any question. This is a
worksheet, not a consent record. Keep it access-appropriate. Discuss boundaries
privately; do not record personal explanations, trauma, health or identity
information, or named safety/preferences records. Store only group-approved,
non-sensitive fictional-content constraints without attribution; omit sensitive
detail by default. Never identify who requested a constraint. Do not copy
private conversations into this worksheet.

Use `unknown`, `proposed`, `agreed`, or `disputed` for decisions. A blank is
unknown, not agreement. Keep disagreements without attribution and preserve
alternatives. A DM may propose options but cannot declare group agreement.
