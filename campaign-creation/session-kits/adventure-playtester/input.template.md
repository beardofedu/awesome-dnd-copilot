## Adventure excerpt: The Bell Beneath Brackenford (fictional sample)

Source: `adventures/bell-beneath-brackenford.md`, revision `demo-head-7f31c2a`.
This deliberately small excerpt is fictional and for demonstration only.

```markdown
# The Bell Beneath Brackenford

Rules: D&D 5e (version not specified). Intended for four level-3 characters;
about one session. Tone: eerie but hopeful.

## Goal and situation

A bell has rung underground each midnight since the old floodgate was sealed.
The village reeve asks the party to find out why before the next flood. The
floodgate chamber is reached through the mill cellar, the riverside culvert, or
the old watchtower stair; any route reaches the same lower sluice.

## Scene: The mill ledger (optional lead)

The miller, Tamsin Reed, has a ledger showing that the bell rope was replaced
three days before the floodgate was sealed. She shares it if treated kindly,
paid 2 gp, or shown the reeve's seal. The ledger points to the lower sluice.
If Tamsin is absent or hostile, the party can still enter by either other route.

## Scene: Flooded archive (required clue)

A brass plate in the archive names the drowned engineer, Ilyra Vale, and says
she sealed the gate to contain a trapped water spirit. A character who searches
the shelves finds it with a DC 13 Intelligence (Investigation) check. On a fail,
the shelves collapse noisily; the plate is not found. The adventure gives no
other way to learn why Ilyra sealed the gate.

## Scene: Lower sluice

The water spirit is bound to the bell rope. It asks the party to cut the rope
and open a narrow spill channel before the next midnight. If the party attacks,
the spirit fights back; it has AC 13 and 22 hit points. The narrow channel is
blocked by a rusted wheel, but the adventure does not say how it can be opened.

If the party opens the spill channel, the water recedes and the bell falls
silent. If the party cuts the rope only, the spirit is released but the village
floods at midnight. The reeve's preferred outcome is to save the village without
harming the spirit; the party may choose another outcome.

## Aftermath

The miller and reeve thank the party if the village is safe. No timing is given
for a rest or other recovery before the spirit's attack, and the adventure does
not say what happens if the party leaves or cannot open the channel.
```
